What changes in v9, in plain words
  • Costco removed from the store list. Target stays as a placeholder until we set it up. Lia will stop mentioning Costco food too.
  • Add on a sale item now puts it in the queue (not Needs). After the next push the sale card is removed, and anything you add is removed from the sales list right away.
  • Check times and Check sales are requests. They go to the listener, which opens Stop & Shop on the engineer PC, reads, and reports back here. They run at the next check-in (8:07, 12:07, 4:07, 7:07) or instantly if you say "push" in the Claude app.
  • Ask Lia gets the mic (same speech-to-text as Quick add), saved chat history, a fix for stray code showing in her replies, and she stops telling you to look at checkout for times.
  • Talk-back voice for Lia is a bigger build and is not in v9.
